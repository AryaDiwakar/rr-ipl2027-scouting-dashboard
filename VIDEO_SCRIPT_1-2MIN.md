## Script (voiceover)

**[0:00–0:12 | Hook + context]**  
"Hi — this is a provisional Rajasthan Royals IPL 2027 scouting shortlist: 10 batters and 10 bowlers. Important context: as of 14 November 2026, official IPL 2027 retention, release, and auction lists are not out yet — so this is a scout-now list, not confirmed auction targets."

**[0:12–0:28 | RR gaps we’re solving for]**  
"Based on our 2022 to 2026 pressure analysis, RR’s biggest needs are: an Indian middle-order option at four to six, an Indian finisher at six-seven, and Indian pace that holds up under pressure in the powerplay and at the death. Secondary needs are a seam-bowling allrounder and attacking spin support — with overseas slots used only for non-redundant specialists."

**[0:28–0:55 | Batting highlights]**  
"On the batting side, the strongest pressure evidence in this list comes from Sikandar Raza — middle-order impact plus off-spin, but he’s mapped to Punjab Kings in 2026, so only if released. Najibullah Zadran and Ryan Burl bring high-sample middle-order profiles, Reeza Hendricks offers top-order intent, and Bevon Jacobs adds finishing power — again conditional on availability. We’ve also included Indian options like Ricky Bhui and Harvik Desai for domestic depth, but they need more recent T20 and pressure-phase evidence."

**[0:55–1:20 | Bowling highlights]**  
"For bowling, Ish Sodhi stands out with a large, high-confidence wrist-spin sample for middle overs, Gudakesh Motie offers control, and Benjamin Sears and Ottneil Baartman provide overseas pace options — including death skills to validate. On the Indian side, RS Ambrish and Naman Tiwari are high-upside developmental picks for the pace gap, but they don’t yet have senior pressure-ball data in our pack, so they’re scout-and-verify."

**[1:20–1:35 | Caveats + compliance]**  
"A few cautions: four players in this list are 2026-team-mapped and should be treated as conditional-only if released, several have low or insufficient pressure samples, and overseas slots are limited. No stats or availability were fabricated — all statuses are unverified until official lists."

**[1:35–1:45 | Close / CTA]**  
"Next step: when official 2027 lists drop, we filter to confirmed released or auction-registered players, fill the evidence gaps, and re-rank for RR fit. That’s the provisional shortlist — ready for scouting."
