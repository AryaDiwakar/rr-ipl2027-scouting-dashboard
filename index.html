How we judged the numbers

We reviewed ball-by-ball performance in the moments that matter most — pressure phases, chasing or setting a target, and recent 2024–26 form.

  • Fair comparisons: players with fewer pressure balls were gently adjusted toward the typical level for their role (a standard small-sample adjustment, m=120 balls), so a short hot streak does not outweigh a larger body of work.
  • Sample depth: Established (120+) Building (60–119) Emerging (under 60 / not in pressure-ball records)
  • Steadiness: for players with 60+ pressure balls, we checked how consistent the numbers stay from match to match (95% range).
  • Different cut-offs tested: pressure thresholds at q50/q60/q70 to make sure picks hold up under sensible definitions of pressure.
Reading the figures: “Adjusted strike rate/economy” reflects performance with that fair small-sample adjustment. Raw figures are shown where helpful. For bowlers, a lower adjusted economy points to better control under pressure.
